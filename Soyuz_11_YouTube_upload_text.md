## Description
On June 30, 1971, the Soyuz 11 capsule made a perfect landing in Kazakhstan. When the recovery team opened the hatch, all three cosmonauts were dead.

Georgy Dobrovolsky, Vladislav Volkov and Viktor Patsayev spent a record 23 days aboard Salyut 1, the world's first space station. On the way home, a valve opened 168 km above the Earth, and the cabin lost its air in seconds. They remain the only people ever to die in space.

This animated story covers the last-minute crew swap, life aboard Salyut 1, the fire, the "hatch open" warning, the fatal valve, and how this tragedy changed spaceflight safety forever.
