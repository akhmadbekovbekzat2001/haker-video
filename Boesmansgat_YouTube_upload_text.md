## Description
In the Northern Cape of South Africa lies Boesmansgat, one of the deepest freshwater caves on Earth: its floor is about 280 meters down, in total darkness.

In 1994, 20-year-old Deon Dreyer blacked out during a dive and vanished into the cave. Ten years later, airline pilot and record-breaking diver Dave Shaw found his body at 270 meters and promised Deon's father he would bring his son home. On January 8, 2005, he went back down.

This animated true story covers both divers, the record dive, the plan, the final dive, and the friend who saw Dave's light stop moving.

Our deepest condolences to their families and friends. The deep forgives no mistakes.
